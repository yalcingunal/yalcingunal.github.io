Mirror Gate

A physics puzzle game with purple and green mirrors.

How to play

Your hero walks on its own. Tap a light wall to place a mirror — the first tap places a purple mirror, the second a green one. Step into one mirror and come out of the other, keeping all your speed. Tap a mirror to remove it, tap the hero to stop or continue (5 times per level). Grab the 🔑 key to open locked doors and reach the yellow exit.
